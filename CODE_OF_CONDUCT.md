# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Expected:**

- Assume the other person is acting in good faith
- Disagree with the idea, not the person
- Say plainly when a rule is wrong, and explain what went wrong
- Accept that a rule you liked may be cut for length

**Not acceptable:**

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Demeaning comments about someone's background or experience
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission
- Sustained disruption, or ignoring repeated feedback

## Scope

Applies in project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when
representing the project publicly.

## Scope note

This project's subject is instructing AI agents to be less sloppy. A contribution
that argues a rule should be longer, without addressing attention budget, is a
legitimate technical disagreement and is treated as one — but "it enforces
discipline" is not by itself an argument for making the file bigger.

## Enforcement

Report conduct you experienced or witnessed to the maintainers via the repository's
security or contact page. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled with the
confidentiality of the reporter.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code may be temporarily or
permanently excluded.
